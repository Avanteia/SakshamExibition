A Step Towards Empowerment

A journey towards excellence — Saksham brings together women of every age for five days of talent, learning and confidence-building.

Our Story

स्वातंत्र्य, परिवर्तन आणि आत्मविश्वास

Saksham — meaning "capable" — is a five-day celebration built entirely around women: their creativity, their talent and their entrepreneurial spirit. From cook-without-fire competitions for the little ones to a traditional fashion show for women, and a full Exhibition cum Sale for women-run businesses, Saksham is a platform for every stage of life to shine.

Held at the Sateri Temple, Mapusa, the event runs from 13 to 17 August 2026, 10:00 AM to 8:00 PM daily, with limited seats to keep every competition personal and welcoming.
